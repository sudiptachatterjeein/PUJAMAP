🚇 Metro fare, timings & commute tips
  • Fare: distance-based. On the Blue Line it starts at ₹5 and reaches roughly ₹25–30 end to end (Dakshineswar–Kavi Subhash). The Green Line has its own slabs and fares get revised, so confirm at the counter.
  • Route, timings and fare: pick your start station above the list, then tap 🚇 Metro route & fare on any pandal. Google Maps' transit view shows the metro route and timings (fare where Google has it).
  • Interchange: Esplanade joins the Blue Line (north–south) and the Green Line (Howrah to Salt Lake).
  • Puja timings: Metro Railway usually announces special services a few days before Shashthi; check mtp.indianrailways.gov.in.
  • Last mile: many South Kolkata and Salt Lake pandals are 1–3 km from a station, so plan an auto or cab for that stretch, and expect closures and one-way flows near big pandals.
🚦 Crowd guide & live traffic
  • This page cannot pull Google's live busyness. Tap 🚦 Map & live crowd to open the place in Google Maps, which shows Popular times and Live status where available, and 🚗 Live traffic map for the traffic layer.
  • Typical pattern (not live data): Shashthi light to moderate, Saptami busy, Ashtami the heaviest especially at night, Navami busy, Dashami lighter at pandals but heavy near immersion routes.
  • Big pandals like Deshapriya Park, Ekdalia and Hindustan Park draw the longest queues, so go early in the afternoon or on an earlier day.
Puja-time tips & help
  • Ashtami and Navami evenings are usually the most crowded; earlier days and weekday afternoons are easier.
  • Metro Railway usually announces special Puja-time services a few days before Shashthi, so check its official notice.
  • Entry and exit gates of big pandals are often at opposite ends, so fix a meeting point with your group first.
  • Wear comfortable shoes and carry a power bank. Emergency in India: call 112.
  • Add to Home Screen: open your browser menu and choose "Add to Home screen" so this map opens like an app.
